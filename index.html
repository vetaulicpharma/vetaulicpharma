Who We Are

About Vet Aulic Pharmaceuticals

We are a dedicated veterinary pharmaceutical company committed to delivering science-backed, high-quality medicines and healthcare solutions for livestock and animals across India.

  • Specialized in veterinary formulations for cattle, buffalo, goat, sheep, and poultry with deep scientific understanding of animal health.
  • Ethically manufactured products meeting strict veterinary pharmaceutical standards with complete batch traceability.
  • Committed to improving livestock productivity, dairy output, and overall animal wellness through reliable, research-based healthcare products.
  • A trusted partner for veterinary practitioners, dairy farmers, and livestock professionals seeking dependable healthcare solutions.
