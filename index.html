About Vet Aulic Pharma

A Commitment to Excellence
in Animal Healthcare

Founded on the principles of scientific rigor, compassionate care, and uncompromising quality, Vet Aulic Pharma has emerged as a trusted name in veterinary pharmaceutical solutions. We dedicate our expertise to the wellbeing of livestock — the backbone of agricultural India — delivering formulations that are clinically effective, safe, and accessible.

Our research-driven approach ensures that every product in our portfolio — from universal liver tonics to species-specific nutritional supplements — meets international pharmaceutical standards. We work hand-in-hand with veterinarians, farmers, and distribution partners to build a healthier livestock ecosystem across the country.
