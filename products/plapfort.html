Prolapse Care P-lap Fort

P-lap Fort

A trusted veterinary formulation developed for the treatment and management of prolapse in cattle. P-lap Fort supports animals affected by vaginal and uterine prolapse, and is suitable for both pre-partum and post-partum cases — helping reduce recurrence and aiding recovery during the critical period around calving.

Recommended Dose Cows & Buffaloes
P-lap Fort Powder
200 gm
Single dose
With Shakkar / Gurh
150 gm
Mixed together

For cows and buffaloes: give a single dose of 200 gm powder along with 150 gm of shakkar (jaggery / gurh), or as directed by a registered veterinarian.

Indications & Benefits

Why Choose P-lap Fort

A targeted solution for prolapse management in cattle — supporting recovery from vaginal and uterine prolapse, before and after calving.

Vaginal Prolapse

Helps in the management and treatment of vaginal prolapse in cattle, supporting the affected tissue and aiding the animal's recovery.

Uterine Prolapse

Indicated for cases of uterine prolapse, helping support the animal through this serious post-calving condition alongside veterinary care.

Pre & Post-Partum Use

Suitable for both pre-partum and post-partum prolapse, offering support during the critical period before and after calving.

Simple to Administer
